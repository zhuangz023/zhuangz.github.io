Research Interests
======

My research focuses on radio astronomy, star formation, and the interstellar medium. I am especially interested in using molecular-line observations to study the physical properties, structure, and evolution of molecular gas in Galactic star-forming regions.

Recent Work
======

My recent work investigates the distribution and properties of molecular gas toward the Monoceros OB1 region. More details and paper links are available on the [Publications](/publications/) page.
