Hi! I am Zi Zhuang, a PhD student in astrophysics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). My research interests include radio astronomy, star formation, and the interstellar medium.

I am currently working on molecular gas in Galactic star-forming regions, with a focus on connecting gas structure and physical conditions to the process of star formation.
